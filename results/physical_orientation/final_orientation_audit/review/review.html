Broadcast orientation review

Each match: compare the PROPOSED view (camera touchline at the bottom) and the 180° ALTERNATIVE with real footage. Use scoreboard clock, period, kits and goalkeepers to identify the half; ignore replays and reverse angles. Decisions are kept in this browser and exported as a JSON file for scripts/import_orientation_reviews.py. Keys: 1 proposed, 2 alternative, 3 can't tell, ←/→ navigate.
